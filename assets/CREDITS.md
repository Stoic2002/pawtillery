# Asset credits

Every third-party asset (image, sound, font) is listed here with its source
and license, and shown on the About screen.

| Asset | Path | Author / source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baloo 2 (variable) | `assets/fonts/Baloo2.ttf` | The Baloo 2 Project Authors (Google Fonts) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/licenses/OFL-Baloo2.txt`) |
| Nunito (variable) | `assets/fonts/Nunito.ttf` | The Nunito Project Authors (Google Fonts) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/licenses/OFL-Nunito.txt`) |

Not third-party, listed so nobody has to wonder: every sound in
`assets/audio/` is synthesized by `tool/audio/generate_audio.dart`, and the
launcher icons are drawn by `tool/icon/app_icon_art.dart`. Both are part of
this project.
